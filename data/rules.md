# Fernhill Sports — Football Betting Rules

Fernhill Sports is a fictional sportsbook. These rules were written for this
project from general industry practice; they are not any real operator's
terms. All amounts are in euro (€).

## 1. General

**R1.1** Football bets are settled on the official result at the end of normal time: 90 minutes plus any stoppage time. Extra time and penalty shoot-outs do not count, unless the market is "To Qualify".

**R1.2** The "To Qualify" market includes extra time and penalty shoot-outs. It is settled on which team progresses to the next round.

**R1.3** A winning bet returns the stake multiplied by the odds (the total return). The maximum total return on any single bet is €100,000.

**R1.4** Bets placed after a match has kicked off on a market that was not open for in-play betting are void.

**R1.5** If a bet was accepted at a price that was obviously wrong (a palpable error), it is settled at the correct price at the time of placement. If no correct price existed, the bet is void.

## 2. Postponed and abandoned matches

**R2.1** If a match does not start within 48 hours of its scheduled kick-off, it is treated as postponed and all bets on it are void.

**R2.2** If a match is abandoned before the end of normal time, bets are void, except on markets whose outcome was already decided when play stopped. For example, "Over 1.5 Goals" wins if two goals had been scored, and an anytime goalscorer bet wins if the player had already scored.

**R2.3** A void single returns the stake.

## 3. Accumulators

**R3.1** An accumulator wins only if every leg wins. One losing leg loses the whole accumulator.

**R3.2** If a leg is void, it counts as a winner at odds of 1.00. The remaining legs settle as normal, and the accumulator's odds are the product of the remaining legs' odds.

**R3.3** If every leg is void, the stake is returned.

## 4. Bet Builder

**R4.1** A Bet Builder combines several selections on the same match at a single combined price. The combined price is not the product of the individual leg prices, because the selections are related.

**R4.2** If a player-based leg is void because the player did not start the match, that leg is removed. The Bet Builder is then settled on the remaining legs at a recalculated price: the original combined price divided by the removed leg's individual price at placement, with a minimum of 1.01.

**R4.3** If the match is abandoned, the whole Bet Builder is void, unless every leg had already been decided as a winner when play stopped, in which case it is settled as a winner.

**R4.4** If every leg of a Bet Builder is void, the stake is returned.

## 5. Player markets

**R5.1** Player bets (goalscorer, shots, cards) stand only if the player starts the match. If the player does not start, the bet is void, even if they come on as a substitute.

**R5.2** Own goals do not count for any goalscorer market.

**R5.3** First Goalscorer: if the first goal is an own goal, it is ignored and the next goal counts. If no player scores, all First Goalscorer bets lose.

## 6. Cash Out

**R6.1** Cash Out may be offered on unsettled singles, accumulators and Bet Builders. It is not available while any unsettled leg's market is suspended, or once the bet has been settled.

**R6.2** The Cash Out value is 95% of the bet's current fair value: stake × odds at placement ÷ the product of the current odds of the legs that are still unsettled. Legs already won count as settled. If a leg is void, the odds at placement are first recalculated as in R3.2 (accumulators) or R4.2 (Bet Builders). The value is rounded down to the nearest cent.

**R6.3** Partial Cash Out lets the customer cash out part of the stake. The remaining stake stays on the bet at the original odds.

**R6.4** A Cash Out cannot be reversed once accepted.

**R6.5** A Cash Out value never exceeds the maximum total return in R1.3.

## 7. Customer service

**R7.1** Customer service will not disclose any information about another customer's account or bets.

**R7.2** Requests to cancel or change an accepted bet, disputes about a settlement, and complaints are referred to the Customer Resolution team. An accepted bet cannot be cancelled or changed except where these rules make it void.

**R7.3** If a customer says they are finding it hard to control their gambling, customer service offers the safer gambling tools (deposit limits, time-outs and self-exclusion) and refers the customer to the Safer Gambling team.

**R7.4** Questions these rules do not cover are referred to a specialist; customer service does not guess at a rule.
